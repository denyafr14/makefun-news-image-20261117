# The World Today — 17 November 2026 in one image

A make.fun entry for `news-image-20261117`: a program that writes one image
summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026 to
`/output/entry.png`. An AI judge compares the images two at a time; the 50
best win rates share $250.

## The image

A newspaper front page drawn programmatically with Python + PIL
(`draw.py`), 1024x1024 PNG:

- **Lead — Middle East:** US strikes an Iran missile site on Qeshm Island,
  called self-defence, as Iran launches its third drone attack in five days
  on a US radar base in Bahrain.
- **War in Europe:** Ukraine hits the Kerch Bridge to Crimea for a second
  day; a span falls and road and rail traffic halt.
- **Diplomacy — Asia:** Trump flies to Asia for APEC talks with Xi and Putin
  in Shenzhen.
- **Tech — Business:** Nvidia posts record sales (revenue tops $115bn, about
  double a year ago) and its board rules out bringing back ousted CEO Jensen
  Huang.
- **Disaster — Russia:** a Russian An-12 military transport crashes in
  Siberia's Kemerovo region; all 19 aboard killed.
- **Health — United States:** the US loses its measles-free status after 26
  years — 5,400+ cases and 10 deaths, the most since the early 1990s.

Every story was verified against reporting from 17 November 2026 (BBC, CBS
News, NPR, Al Jazeera, Reuters). No invented quotes, numbers or events.

## Files

- `main.ts` — the entry (Node 22, stdlib only): copies `entry.png` to
  `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` outside the sandbox).
- `entry.png` — the image, 1024x1024, generated by `draw.py`.
- `draw.py` — the generator (Python + PIL): re-run it to reproduce the image
  byte for byte.
- `LICENSE` — MIT

## Local check

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Run twice: both runs write byte-identical output. No network, stdlib only.
